WobbleWorks — Privacy Policy

WobbleWorks is a building and science game for children, made by Banx Games. We built it so that it collects nothing about children. This policy explains what that means in practice.

What WobbleWorks keeps, and where

  • Everything children make and do — inventor names and looks, progress, inventions, experiments, photos and settings — is saved only on the device the game is played on.
  • There are no accounts for children and no sign-in. An inventor name can be anything; we suggest a first name or a nickname.
  • WobbleWorks does not upload, sell or share any of it. We cannot see it.

What WobbleWorks never does

  • No advertising, no ad tracking and no behavioural profiling.
  • No analytics or tracking of how children play.
  • No chat, no messages and no contact with other players.
  • No location, camera or microphone access. Photo Mode pictures are made inside the game and saved only where a grown-up chooses.

Things that go over the internet

  • The game downloads its own pictures, sounds and levels. Once installed it works offline.
  • Spoken narration uses the device's own text-to-speech voices. Some browsers and devices use an online voice service for this; it receives only Bolt's lines, never anything a child typed. Narration can be turned off in Settings.
  • Buying or restoring the full game happens behind a grown-up gate:
  • in an app: through the App Store or Google Play, under their privacy terms
  • on the web: through the WobbleWorks parent account service, which uses a grown-up's email address only to send a sign-in code and to remember the purchase. No information about children is sent.

Backups

A grown-up can export a backup file and import it again, for example on a new device. The file stays wherever the grown-up saves it. It never contains purchase details.

Children's privacy

WobbleWorks is designed to meet children's privacy rules such as COPPA (United States), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and the GDPR, by collecting no personal information from children. If you believe information about a child has reached us anyway, contact us and we will delete it.

Grown-ups' rights

If you used the web parent account service, you can ask us to see or delete the email address and purchase record we hold. Contact us at [contact email].
